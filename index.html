About Me

Daniel Steven Setyawan

Turning Ideas Into Interactive Designs

I'm Daniel Steven Setyawan (19), a first-year student in the Bachelor of Informatics Engineering Program, Faculty of Engineering, Universitas Negeri Surabaya. I have a deep passion for technology and informatics, especially in software development, network systems, and cybersecurity.

Since high school, I've also been interested in graphic design, UI/UX, and front-end development, which helps me combine creativity and functionality in my projects. My enthusiasm for technology and design drives me to keep learning, innovating, and creating practical and creative solutions through every project I work on.

I specialize in transforming creative ideas into interactive and visually engaging designs. By combining my skills in front-end development, UI/UX, and graphic design, I aim to build digital experiences that are not only functional and responsive, but also aesthetically meaningful and enjoyable for users.
